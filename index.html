Never type these into an AI tool

Not even to check something. Once you type it, you cannot take it back.

Stay safe

Where to check instead

Go back to where the information really comes from. Asking the same AI again, or a different AI, is not a check.

Stay safe

Warning signs in an AI reply

Slow down and check when you see any of these.

  • Exact numbers or percentages you never gave it.
  • Phrases like “as per the rules” or “experts say”, with no document named.
  • A name, job title or approval you did not mention.
  • A day and a date that do not match on the calendar.
  • Helpful extras that are not in your source, like “tools will be provided”.
